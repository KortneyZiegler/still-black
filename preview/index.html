The Six Featured Subjects

DOCUMENTARY CAST ARCHIVE & BIOGRAPHIES
PASTOR & DAD

Carl Madgett

Chicago, IL
Pastor, Husband & Father

Pastor Carl Madgett is a loving father of twins, husband, and spiritual shepherd in Chicago. In STILL BLACK, Carl speaks intimately about the joys and responsibilities of Black fatherhood, balancing community ministry, and actively dismantling the harmful myth that transgender identity is incompatible with pastoral calling and deep Christian faith.

Theme: Faith, Fatherhood & Ministry Module 02 Spotlight
ELDER & ARTIST

Ethan Young

Toledo, OH
Visual Artist, Elder & Historian

Ethan Young brings rare, grounding intergenerational wisdom to the documentary as an elder and visual artist. Reflecting on decades of transition, creative practice, and community survival in the Midwest, Ethan’s narrative provides essential historical perspective on Black trans longevity, bodily peace, and thriving across an entire lifespan.

Theme: Intergenerational Longevity Module 01 Spotlight
LEGAL PIONEER

Kylar Broadus, Esq.

Columbia, MO
Civil Rights Attorney & Professor

Kylar Broadus, Esq. is a historic civil rights attorney, law professor, and founder of the Trans People of Color Coalition (TPOCC). Kylar made American history as the very first openly transgender person to testify before the United States Senate (in support of the Employment Non-Discrimination Act), dedicating his life to civil rights advocacy, economic justice, and institutional change.

Theme: Civil Rights & US Senate Testimony Module 03 Spotlight
YOUNG POET

James Welch

Evanston, IL
Spoken Word Poet & Musician

James Welch represents the electrifying creative voice of young Black trans masculinity. As a spoken-word artist, lyricist, and musician, James articulates the vulnerable realities of youthful self-discovery, romantic intimacy, navigating social expectations, and claiming one's personal truth with uncompromised artistic courage.

Theme: Youth Voice & Spoken Word Module 01 Spotlight
VETERAN

Nicholas Rashad

Chicago, IL
Military Veteran & Advocate

Nicholas Ishmael Rashad is an American armed forces veteran and community advocate. In STILL BLACK, Nicholas discusses military discipline, honor, navigating traditional institutional structures, and living as an unapologetic Black trans man with self-respect, quiet dignity, and principled pride.

Theme: Public Service & Self-Determination Module 03 Spotlight
FAITH LEADER

Rev. Louis Mitchell

Springfield, MA
Ordained Minister & Interfaith Pioneer

Rev. Louis Mitchell is an ordained minister, spiritual counselor, and nationally recognized interfaith pioneer. For decades, Rev. Mitchell has worked at the intersections of spiritual care, racial justice, and transgender affirmation, creating sacred sanctuary and healing ancestral spiritual trauma for Black LGBTQ+ individuals nationwide.

Theme: Sacred Belonging & Pastoral Care Module 02 Spotlight

About the Director: Dr. Kortney Ryan Ziegler

Scholar, Artist & Technologist • First African American Studies Ph.D. (Northwestern University)

Dr. Kortney Ryan Ziegler is an award-winning artist, scholar, and social entrepreneur. A cornerstone of Black queer cinema, Dr. Ziegler made history as the first person to graduate with a Ph.D. in African American Studies from Northwestern University. His multidisciplinary work sits at the intersection of media, economic empowerment, and queer liberation.
