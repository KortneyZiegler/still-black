The Film

Faith, family, love, and work, told by the men who lived it.

An intimate portrait of six Black trans men speaking candidly about who they are and how the world sees them.

Deacon & Father

Carl Madgett

Chicago, IL

A deacon and father of twin daughters, building a family and a church community rooted in love.

Artist

Ethan Young

Toledo, OH

Reflects on disability, Black manhood, love, and the spiritual practice of tattoos and piercings.

Civil Rights Attorney

Kylar Broadus

Columbia, MO

Pushed out of corporate America after transitioning, he built a career fighting for trans legal rights.

Poet & Musician

James Welch

Evanston, IL

A poet who has “always written to stay alive,” finding himself through hip-hop and spoken word.

Student & Uncle

Nicholas Ishmael Rashad

Chicago, IL

Came home to Chicago’s south suburbs to transition near family, and reflects on siblings and acceptance.

Ordained Minister

Rev. Louis Mitchell

Springfield, MA

On letting spiritual transition lead the physical, and giving loved ones time to come along.
